Roasted Chicken Rub

Description

This seasoning for roast chicken is a herby, smoky spice blend that complements the chicken perfectly.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon celery flakes
  • 1 tablespoon kosher salt
  • 1 tablespoon paprika
  • 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon onion powder
  • 1 tablespoon ground thyme
  • 2 teaspoons dried sage
  • 1 ½ teaspoons ground black pepper
  • 1 ½ teaspoons dried rosemary
  • ½ teaspoon cayenne pepper

Steps

  1. Stir celery flakes, salt, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, thyme, sage, black pepper, rosemary, and cayenne pepper together in a bowl.
  2. Transfer spice mixture to a spice grinder or food processor; blend until smooth.
